# LifeOps Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Foundation
Django project, authentication, private dashboard, demo seed data, tests, documentation.

## Milestone 2 — Dashboard & CRUD
Complete front-end CRUD, dashboard widget preferences, improved navigation, drag-and-drop ordering with SortableJS.

## Milestone 3 — Daily Planning
Calendar views, work/school/personal schedule filters, priorities, weekly planning, reminders.

## Milestone 4 — Finance
Transactions, monthly budget progress, savings goals, debt payoff planner, CSV import/export, charts.

## Milestone 5 — Wellness & Meals
Habit check-ins, workout templates, exercise logs, measurements, recipe library, grocery-list generation.

## Milestone 6 — Career & Student Tools
Application pipeline board, interview checklist, resume notes, course tracker, GPA planner, workload dashboard.

## Milestone 7 — Home Operations
Low-stock alerts, purchase history, receipts, warranties, shared household inventory.

## Milestone 8 — Collaboration
Read receipts, groups, households/classes/teams, shared schedules/tasks, granular permissions, activity feed.

## Milestone 9 — Product Polish
Global search, notifications center, accessibility audit, deployment, screenshots/demo GIF, case study, API.
